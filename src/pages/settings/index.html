Spaces

Each Space keeps its own tabs, look and pinned sites. Switch from the sidebar or with Ctrl+Shift+1…9. Spaces that aren't isolated share cookies: they organize tabs but don't separate your data.

Sync

Vexa has no server. Sync keeps one encrypted file in a folder you choose — put it in OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or Syncthing on each device. Bookmarks, passwords, autofill and settings sync; history, tabs and downloads stay on each device.

Extensions Advanced

Load an unpacked Chrome extension (a folder with manifest.json). Most extensions that change pages work; toolbar popups don't yet. Only add extensions you trust — they can read the pages you visit.

Compatibility Advanced

For sites that are strict about which browser you use. The details are in docs/COMPATIBILITY.md.

Google sign-in

Present the Chrome brand to GoogleOn Google's own sites only, Vexa adds “Google Chrome” to the browser brands it reports. Vexa is built on Chromium but isn't Chrome, so this is a compatibility claim. Other sites always see the honest brands.
“Sign in with Google” buttons on other sitesLets these buttons use your Google cookies even though Shields blocks cross-site cookies. Only Google's sign-in address is allowed; with this off, the buttons stop working.
If Google says “This browser or app may not be secure”Google decides which browsers may sign in, and Vexa can't override that. A passkey, “Sign in with Microsoft/Apple” on other sites, or Google's own apps may still work. See the Google sign-in part of docs/COMPATIBILITY.md.
